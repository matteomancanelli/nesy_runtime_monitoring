\section{Automata-based Neuro-Symbolic Monitoring: DeepDFA}
\label{sec:deepdfa}

\subsection{Architecture}
\label{sec:deepdfa-arch}

DeepDFA \cite{deepdfa_ecai2024} keeps the automaton of the symbolic paradigm but replaces its explicit state walk with a differentiable, tensorized one; we adopt the same forward formulation used for predictive monitoring of business processes by \cite{mezini2025neuro}. In its original formulation DeepDFA is a \emph{probabilistic relaxation} of a DFA: a probabilistic finite automaton whose transition logits and acceptance logits are real-valued and \emph{learnable}. Temperature-scaled softmax constrains each transition row to the probability simplex, temperature-scaled sigmoid produces the acceptance vector, and annealing drives both toward the one-hot/Boolean limit. This is distinct from the Gumbel--Softmax estimator used by \cite{mezini2025neuro} to sample differentiable near-symbolic \emph{inputs} for a fixed automaton \cite{gumb_sftmx_1,gumb_sftmx_2}. Accordingly, starting from the minimal DFA $A_\varphi = (2^\calP, Q, q_0, \delta, F)$ of Section~\ref{sec:ltlf}, the general object is a three-way tensor $T \in [0,1]^{|Q| \times |2^\calP| \times |Q|}$ that is row-stochastic in its last index, $\sum_{q'} T[q, \sigma, q'] = 1$, together with an acceptance vector $v_o \in [0,1]^{|Q|}$. When the automaton is instead \emph{compiled} from a known \ltlf formula --- the setting of this paper's monitoring experiments, and the fixed knowledge layer of \cite{mezini2025neuro} --- these quantities are fixed to their exact one-hot values, $T \in \{0,1\}^{|Q| \times |2^\calP| \times |Q|}$ with $T[q, \sigma, q'] = 1$ iff $\delta(q, \sigma) = q'$, and $v_o \in \{0,1\}^{|Q|}$ with $v_o[q] = 1$ iff $q \in F$. Learning them is unnecessary for monitoring a known specification; a separate real-valued parameterization would instead be needed for the specification-adaptation problem deferred to Section~\ref{sec:conclusion}. In either case the monitor state is no longer a single automaton state but a row vector $\mathbf{q}_t \in [0,1]^{|Q|}$ over states, initialized to the one-hot vector $\mathbf{q}_0 = v_i$ of $q_0$. Writing $T_\sigma = T[\,\cdot\,, \sigma, \cdot\,] \in [0,1]^{|Q| \times |Q|}$ for the (row-stochastic) transition matrix of symbol $\sigma$, a step is a single vector--matrix product
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{q}_t = \mathbf{q}_{t-1}\, T_{\sigma_t},
  \qquad
  \mathrm{acc}_t = \mathbf{q}_t \cdot v_o,
  \label{eq:deepdfa-crisp}
\end{equation}
where $\mathrm{acc}_t \in [0,1]$ is the acceptance score of the prefix read so far. The following elementary invariant records exactly what is preserved by the tensorization.

\begin{proposition}[Crisp equivalence]
\label{prop:deepdfa-crisp-equivalence}
Let $\sigma_{1:t}=\sigma_1\cdots\sigma_t$ be a crisp prefix and let $\mathbf q_t$ be obtained from Equation~\eqref{eq:deepdfa-crisp} using the compiled one-hot tensor. Then
\[
  \mathbf q_t=e_{\delta^*(q_0,\sigma_{1:t})},
\]
where $e_q$ is the indicator vector of state $q$. Consequently the fixed DeepDFA monitor and the symbolic DFA visit the same state after every prefix and return identical early and final verdicts.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At $t=0$, $\mathbf q_0=e_{q_0}$. If $\mathbf q_{t-1}=e_q$, the compiled row $T[q,\sigma_t,\cdot]$ has its unique one at $\delta(q,\sigma_t)$, hence $\mathbf q_t=e_qT_{\sigma_t}=e_{\delta(q,\sigma_t)}$. Induction proves the invariant. Trap, accepting-sink, and final-state membership are functions of this same reached state, so the verdicts coincide.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Soft Observations and Differentiability}
\label{sec:deepdfa-soft}

Beyond crisp monitoring, the tensorization admits probabilistic observations. For $\mathbf p\in[0,1]^{\calP}$ and valuation $\sigma\subseteq\calP$, define the independent-Bernoulli weight
\begin{equation}
  w_{\mathbf p}(\sigma)
  =\prod_{a\in\sigma}p_a\prod_{a\in\calP\setminus\sigma}(1-p_a).
  \label{eq:valuation-weight}
\end{equation}
If $g_{q,q'}$ is the disjunction of the guards on all edges from $q$ to $q'$, the exact expected transition is
\begin{equation}
  M(\mathbf p)[q,q']
  =\sum_{\sigma\models g_{q,q'}}w_{\mathbf p}(\sigma).
  \label{eq:expected-transition-wmc}
\end{equation}
Thus a per-atom probability vector produced by a perceptor or sensor replaces one-hot symbol selection while leaving the forward update unchanged:
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{q}_t = \mathbf{q}_{t-1}\, M(\mathbf{p}_t),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{acc}_t = \mathbf{q}_t \cdot v_o.
  \label{eq:deepdfa-soft}
\end{equation}
The general construction and its state-distribution semantics are already established for symbolic automata by NeSyA \cite{NesyA}. We record the specialization needed here to make the assumptions and the correctness of our LTLf monitor explicit.

\begin{proposition}[Exact guard WMC]
\label{prop:deepdfa-exact-wmc}
Let $\mathcal C_{q,q'}$ be a pairwise-disjoint cube cover of $g_{q,q'}$. For a cube $c$, let $c^+$ and $c^-$ be its required-true and required-false atoms and set
\[
  w_{\mathbf p}(c)=\prod_{a\in c^+}p_a\prod_{a\in c^-}(1-p_a).
\]
Then $M(\mathbf p)[q,q']=\sum_{c\in\mathcal C_{q,q'}}w_{\mathbf p}(c)$ equals Equation~\eqref{eq:expected-transition-wmc} for every Boolean guard. Moreover, $M(\mathbf p)$ is row-stochastic and differentiable in $\mathbf p$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Each cube denotes a cylinder set of complete valuations and its product is the total Bernoulli weight of that set. The cubes are disjoint and their union is exactly the models of $g_{q,q'}$, so their weights sum to Equation~\eqref{eq:expected-transition-wmc}. For fixed $q$, determinism and completeness of the DFA make the outgoing guards a partition of $2^{\calP}$; therefore $\sum_{q'}M(\mathbf p)[q,q']=\sum_{\sigma\subseteq\calP}w_{\mathbf p}(\sigma)=1$. Each entry is a finite multilinear polynomial in $\mathbf p$ and is therefore differentiable.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}[Trace-marginal semantics]
\label{prop:deepdfa-trace-marginal}
Let $\Sigma_1,\ldots,\Sigma_L$ be independent random valuations, with $\Pr(\Sigma_t=\sigma)=w_{\mathbf p_t}(\sigma)$, and propagate Equation~\eqref{eq:deepdfa-soft} with the exact matrices of Equation~\eqref{eq:expected-transition-wmc}. Then, for every $q\in Q$,
\[
  \mathbf q_t[q]
  =\Pr\!\left(\delta^*(q_0,\Sigma_{1:t})=q\right).
\]
Consequently $\mathbf q_L\cdot v_o=\Pr(\Sigma_{1:L}\models\varphi)$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The claim is immediate at $t=0$. Assuming it at $t-1$, the law of total probability and independence of $\Sigma_t$ from the preceding cells give
\[
  \Pr(\delta^*(q_0,\Sigma_{1:t})=q')
  =\sum_{q\in Q}\mathbf q_{t-1}[q]M(\mathbf p_t)[q,q'],
\]
which is exactly the $q'$ component of Equation~\eqref{eq:deepdfa-soft}. Summing the final distribution over $F$, equivalently taking its inner product with $v_o$, yields the acceptance marginal.
\end{proof}

Because the cube weights are smooth, the fixed monitor is differentiable with respect to its observations: a verdict loss can flow through it into an upstream perceptor without changing the specification. Making the specification itself learnable is a separate construction requiring parameterized transitions or guards, an adaptation objective, and a discrete extraction policy; we defer that problem to Section~\ref{sec:conclusion}. In this paper all evaluated traces are crisp, where $M(\mathbf p_t)$ collapses to $T_{\sigma_t}$ and Proposition~\ref{prop:deepdfa-crisp-equivalence} applies.
